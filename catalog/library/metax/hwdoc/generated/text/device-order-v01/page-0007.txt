MACA_VISIBLE_DEVICES 配置工具介绍
/opt/maca/tools/communication/inspector/mx-cmi device_order -h
2.3 使用示例
# TP8 + DP2
/opt/maca/tools/communication/inspector/mx-cmi device_order --tp 8 --dp 2
# TP4 + DP4
/opt/maca/tools/communication/inspector/mx-cmi device_order --tp 4 --dp 4
# MoE ：TP2 + EP8
/opt/maca/tools/communication/inspector/mx-cmi device_order --tp 2 --ep 8
2.4 输出说明
标准输出为可直接使用的 MACA 卡序环境变量配置，可以导入到当前运行环境：
• MACA_DEVICE_ORDER=PCI_BUS_ID
• MACA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=…
示例如下：
# TP4 + DP4
$ /opt/maca/tools/communication/inspector/mx-cmi device_order --tp 4 --dp 4
MACA_DEVICE_ORDER=PCI_BUS_ID
MACA_VISIBLE_DEVICES="0,1,2,3,6,5,4,7,10,11,8,9,14,13,12,15"
# TP8 + DP2
$ /opt/maca/tools/communication/inspector/mx-cmi device_order --tp 8 --dp 2
MACA_DEVICE_ORDER=PCI_BUS_ID
MACA_VISIBLE_DEVICES="0,1,2,3,6,5,4,7,10,9,8,11,14,15,12,13"
